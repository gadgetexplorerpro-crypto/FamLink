Privacy First • Zero Covert Surveillance

1. Introduction

FamLink (also referred to as "TrackView", "we", "our", or "the App") is a family safety, live GPS location sharing, SafeZone geofence, and parental control application designed to help family members stay connected safely and transparently. We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of every family member.

Core Principle: FamLink does NOT support covert surveillance, hidden monitoring, or secret background recording. Any location sharing, camera transmission, or audio session requires mutual consent and active, visible on-screen indicators on the transmitting device.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information strictly necessary to provide family safety and device connection features:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your Google user ID, display name, email address, and profile photo URL. We never access your Google password or private Google Workspace data.
  • Precise Location Data (GPS): With your explicit runtime permission, TrackView collects real-time GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, accuracy radius, and speed) to display your location to approved family members, calculate SafeZone entries and departures, and provide emergency SOS alerts.
  • Connected Device Information: Device model, device name, battery percentage, charging state, online/offline status, and unique cryptographic device tokens used for secure peer-to-peer pairing.
  • Family Safety Configuration: Custom SafeZones (geofence center and radius), bedtime schedules, screen time limits, and safety alert histories.
  • Family Messaging: In-app chat messages, ETA shares, and check-ins exchanged exclusively between authorized members of your family group.

3. Camera and Microphone Privacy

TrackView supports consent-gated remote camera and two-way voice sessions between authorized family devices.

  • Explicit Approval Required: A remote camera or voice session cannot start automatically. The receiving device must receive an incoming prompt and the user must explicitly tap "Approve".
  • Continuous Visual Indicator: While any camera or microphone session is active, a prominent glowing indicator ("LIVE CAMERA ACTIVE" or "LIVE VOICE ACTIVE") and an instant "Stop" button remain visible on screen at all times.
  • Zero Covert Recording: Audio and video streams are transmitted peer-to-peer using WebRTC (STUN/TURN) and are NOT recorded, stored, or analyzed on our servers.

4. How We Use and Share Information

  • We use your information solely to deliver the safety, location sharing, and messaging services you request.
  • No Selling of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade personal data or location histories with third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers.
  • Service Providers: We use Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) for secure cloud infrastructure and Google Identity Services for authentication.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

TrackView is designed for family use, where parents or legal guardians set up and supervise accounts for minors. We require verifiable parental consent before enabling location sharing on a child's device. Children's data is never used for commercial profiling or advertising.

6. Security and End-to-End Encryption

All communication between devices and our backend is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption and zero-trust Firestore security rules. Peer-to-peer media streams are secured using WebRTC DTLS-SRTP encryption.

7. Your Data Rights & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Emergency Ghost Mode: Instantly pause all GPS transmission with one tap from the Home or Settings screen.
  • Revoke Device Access: Remove any connected phone or family member at any time from the Devices screen.
  • Export Location Data: Export your stored SafeZone and family records as formatted JSON.
  • Permanent Account & Data Deletion: You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated cloud data directly inside the App (Settings → Delete Account) or online via our dedicated deletion portal.
