1. Overview of Data Deletion Rights

Under Google Play's User Data policy and global privacy standards (GDPR / CCPA), users have the right to request the deletion of their user account as well as specific categories of personal data collected during app usage.

You do not need to delete your entire account if you only wish to purge historical location points, reset custom SafeZones, or remove individual paired device connections.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

Option 1: In-App Direct Data Management (Instant)

  1. Open the FamLink (TrackView) application.
  2. Delete GPS Location History: Go to Settings → Emergency Ghost Mode or toggle off location sharing. Live coordinates are instantly cleared from cloud view.
  3. Delete Custom SafeZones: Open the SafeZones screen, select any geofence zone, and tap Delete SafeZone.
  4. Unpair Devices: Open the Devices tab, tap the paired device card, and select Remove / Unpair Device.
  5. Delete Entire Account: Open Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account & Wipe Cloud Data.
