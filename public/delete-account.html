1. How to Delete Your Account (3 Easy Methods)

Method A: Inside the FamLink / TrackView App (Instant & Automatic)

The fastest way to delete your account and erase all data immediately:

  1. Open the FamLink (TrackView) app on your Android phone.
  2. From Login Screen: Tap the Delete Account link at the bottom of the sign-in card, sign in with your Google Account to verify identity, and confirm deletion.
  3. From Inside App: Go to the Settings screen, scroll to the Danger Zone, and tap Delete Account & Wipe Cloud Data.
  4. Upon confirmation, all cloud documents, pairing keys, and GPS logs are permanently wiped instantly from Firebase Firestore.
